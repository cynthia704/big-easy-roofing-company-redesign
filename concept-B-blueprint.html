The three calls we get most

Roof Problems in This City
Are Rarely Just Roof Problems

Between the humidity, the wind, and the claims process, a New Orleans roof fails in specific ways. Here is what usually brings people to us.

The Ceiling Is Wet

By the time water shows up inside, the leak has usually been running for weeks. We find the entry point, not just the stain, and tarp the same day.

The Storm Left Something Behind

Lifted shingles and cracked flashing are easy to miss from the ground and easy for an adjuster to under-scope. We photograph every slope.

The Claim Came Back Short

A denial or a low number is usually a documentation problem. We give your adjuster measurements and photographs to work from.
