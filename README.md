# Big Easy Roofing Company — Homepage Concepts

Three homepage directions for **bigeasyroofingcompany.com**, built for internal
review. Open `index.html` (or the live link) and pick one.

| Concept | Direction |
|---|---|
| **A — Storm Watch** | Dark, cinematic, emergency-led |
| **B — The Blueprint** | Light, technical, expert |
| **C — Front Porch** | Warm, local, editorial |

These are working pages, not flat mockups. Scroll them, hover things, click the
interactive parts, and check them on a phone.

## Before you judge the copy

- Review quotes are **placeholders** until the real Google reviews are pulled.
- The licence number, the Unlimited Wind Warranty terms and the "30+ years"
  claim are **unconfirmed** and bracketed in the build.
- The Google and Facebook badges assert a **5-star rating nobody has verified**
  against the live Business Profile.
- All photography is the company's own, taken from its media library. None of
  it is stock.

## Notes

- Pages are `noindex, nofollow`.
- No build step. Plain HTML, one file per concept, CSS keyframes plus
  IntersectionObserver. No animation library.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected throughout.
- Full working notes, the bug log and the open questions live in the project
  folder, not in this repo.
