Is it time?

Six Signs a Repair
Will Not Hold Any Longer

If two or more of these describe your roof, replacing it is usually cheaper over five years than repeat repairs.

The Roof Is Over 20 Years OldAsphalt shingle in this climate rarely makes 25. Heat and humidity age it faster than the label allows for.
Granules in the GuttersThose granules are the shingle's sunscreen. Once they wash off, what is left cooks and cracks.
You Have Patched It More Than TwiceRepeat repairs on one slope usually mean the underlayment has failed, not the shingle.
The Deck Feels Soft UnderfootSoft decking means water has been sitting in the structure. No shingle fixes that.
Daylight Through the Attic BoardsIf light gets in, so does water, and so does the wind that lifts the next row of shingles.
A Named Storm Went Over ItWind damage is often invisible from the ground. It still costs the roof its ability to shed the next one.

SystemTypical lifeWind ratingBest for

What moves the price

Why Nobody Can Quote
Your Roof Over the Phone

Anyone who gives you a number before seeing the roof is guessing, and the guess gets corrected once the tear-off starts. These are the things that actually set the price.

01

Square Footage and Pitch

A steep roof is slower and needs more safety rigging. Two houses the same size can differ by a third on labour alone.

02

Decking Condition

Unknowable until the old roof is off. We quote a per-sheet rate up front so the number cannot move on you.

03

Material Choice

The spread between architectural shingle and standing seam metal is the single biggest lever you control.

04

Penetrations and Detail

Chimneys, skylights, dormers and valleys are where roofs leak and where the labour goes.

05

Access and Tear-Off Layers

Two existing layers means twice the disposal. Tight access means everything moves by hand.
