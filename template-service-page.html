A replacement is the one roofing job where the cheap quote and the honest quote look identical on paper. The difference is what happens after the old roof comes off, and whether that was priced before you signed.

When a Repair Will Not Hold Any Longer

If two or more of these describe your roof, replacing it is usually cheaper over five years than repeat repairs:

  • The roof is over 20 years old. Asphalt shingle in this climate rarely makes 25.
  • Granules are collecting in the gutters. That is the shingle's sunscreen washing off.
  • You have patched the same slope more than twice, which usually means the underlayment has failed rather than the shingle.
  • The deck feels soft underfoot, so water has been sitting in the structure.
  • Daylight shows through the attic boards.
  • A named storm went over it and nobody has been up since.

What a Replacement Actually Covers

Every line below is in the written estimate before anyone climbs a ladder. Nothing here is an add-on discovered halfway through.

  • Full tear-off to the deck. No layering over the existing roof.
  • Decking inspection, with soft sheets replaced at a per-sheet rate quoted up front.
  • Ice and water barrier at valleys, eaves and every penetration.
  • Synthetic underlayment across the full deck.
  • New flashing at chimneys, walls, valleys and vents. Reusing old flashing is where cheap quotes save money.
  • Ridge ventilation, because bad ventilation cooks a roof from the inside and voids most manufacturer warranties.
  • Shingles and ridge cap in the colour and profile you picked.
  • Cleanup and magnet sweep of the yard, beds and driveway.

Why Nobody Can Quote Your Roof Over the Phone

Anyone who gives you a number before seeing the roof is guessing, and the guess gets corrected once the tear-off starts. Five things set the price: square footage and pitch, decking condition, material choice, the number of penetrations and detail areas, and how many layers have to come off.

The inspection is free and the estimate is line-itemed, so you can hold it next to any other quote in New Orleans and see exactly where they differ.
