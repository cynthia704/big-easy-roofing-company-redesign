Soffit photography for /soffit-services/.
Source: Pexels. Pexels License - free to use, commercial use allowed,
no attribution required. Ids kept so any image can be traced back.

Every one of these was opened and looked at before it was picked. The set it
replaced was borrowed from the residential library and none of it showed
soffit: a standing seam metal roof was captioned "Soffit Panel and Intake
Vent", underlayment was captioned "Soffit Installation", and a pitch gauge on
shingles was captioned "Fascia Board".

Cropped to the slot rather than downloaded raw. Six of the ten sources are
portrait and every slot on the page is landscape, so a raw file would have
been centre cropped into a narrow band. On sof-panel-vent that band was plain
timber with the vent cropped out.

Ten sources, ten slots, no reuse.
