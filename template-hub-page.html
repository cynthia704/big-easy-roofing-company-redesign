What Actually Goes on Your Roof

A roof is not one product, it is four layers that have to be right in order. Most of them are also what GAF requires before the WindProven Limited Wind Warranty applies — fifteen years, no maximum wind speed, no named-storm exclusion. Leave one out and the roof still works. It just is not covered the same way.

Layer 01
01

Starter Strip, Leak Barrier and Deck Protection

Three things go onto a bare deck before a single shingle does, and you will never see any of them again. Starter strip seals the eave edge so wind cannot get a finger under the first course. Leak barrier goes into the valleys and around every penetration. Deck protection covers everything else, and it is what keeps your ceilings dry between tear-off and the last nail.

02

Flashing at Every Penetration

Roofs almost never fail in the open field. They fail where something comes through them: a vent stack, a chimney, a skylight, the wall of a dormer. Every penetration gets new metal. We do not re-use flashing that was already bent once, because the second bend is where the leak starts.

New metal every time, never re-usedThe first place we look on a leak call

Before you file

Two Things About Louisiana
That Catch Homeowners Out

Neither of these is about roofing. Both change what a storm-damaged roof ends up costing you.

01 Timing

The Clock Starts at the Storm,
Not at the Stain

Louisiana puts a time limit on claims for storm damage, and it runs from the date of the loss, not from the day you noticed the ceiling.

Damage before July 1, 2024 1 year
Damage on or after July 1, 2024 2 years

The practical version: if a named storm has been over your roof and nobody has been up there since, do not assume the window has closed. Check the date of the storm against your policy before you decide it is too late. Your policy can set its own terms on top of the statute, so read it.

[Verified September 3, 2026 against Louisiana Civil Code art. 3493.12. Have the firm’s attorney sign off on this wording before launch, and re-check it annually.]

02 Payout

Older Roofs Are Often Paid
at Actual Cash Value

That means depreciation comes off the payout. A twenty-year-old roof can be fully covered and still leave a gap between what the insurer pays and what the job costs. Knowing which one your policy carries changes how you plan.

Replacement Cost Value Pays what the new roof costs today
Actual Cash Value Pays that, minus age and wear. The gap is yours

We are not insurance adjusters and we will not tell you your claim is guaranteed. What we do is document the damage thoroughly enough that your adjuster has everything they need to price it properly.
