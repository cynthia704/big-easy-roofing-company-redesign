Unlimited Wind Warranty

The live page calls it the company's own warranty, for the life of the roof, transferable, covering any hurricane. Rebuilt on GAF's verified WindProven terms: 15 years, no maximum wind speed, no named-storm exclusion, on qualifying Timberline systems.

Worked example: /unlimited-wind-warranty/
Live H1 kept; the "unlimited" is explained as wind speed. GAF certification level still open for Brian
Open the warranty page

One thing worth knowing. The credential bar under the hero drops the GAF WindProven line on the commercial and soffit hubs. WindProven is a Timberline shingle warranty, so claiming it on a flat commercial roof would be false, and it contradicted the panel further down that page. Each hub now carries only the claims that are true for it.

Before you judge the copy

  • Placeholder Review quotes are placeholders until we pull the real Google reviews.
  • Unverified The licence number, the Unlimited Wind Warranty terms and the "30+ years" claim are all still unconfirmed and bracketed in the build.
  • Unverified The Google and Facebook badges assert a 5-star rating nobody has checked against the live Business Profile.
